# Peiron Race: authenticity checklist

The goal is that a Nagasaki child who has watched the 長崎ペーロン選手権大会 recognises it.
Each item lists the real-world fact (with a source), what the game did before, and what it does now.
✅ = matches the real race, ⚠️ = simplified on purpose (with the reason), ❌ = still wrong.

## Sources
- 長崎市ペーロン協会 (at-nagasaki.jp/dmo/summary/peron): 45尺 (13.6 m) boat, ≤30 crew (≤26 paddlers + 太鼓, ドラ, 舵手, あか汲み), 1,150 m return course (630 m out / 520 m back), paddles longer at bow and stern because the hull is bow-shaped, cedar hull, each team paints its own mark on the bow, spectators watch from the roof of 松が枝国際ターミナル.
- 海と日本PROJECT in ながさき / KTN: venue 松が枝国際観光船ふ頭, 往復1,150 m (往路630 m・復路520 m).
- 井上しげひさ report, 2023: the course runs 松ヶ枝国際観光埠頭前から沖合までの往復 (from in front of the pier out to sea and back). 野母崎 won in 4:53.98, pulling clear right after rounding the turn buoy.
- tabirai / ナガジン: kakegoe 「ヨーヤーサー」/「ヨーイサー」, rhythm 「ドーン、ドン、ジャーン、ジャン！」, fast and intense.
- コトバンク (マイペディア): paddlers face forward and pull the water from front to back with a ~1 m 櫂, and the drum and gong set the timing and pitch.
- 長崎経済新聞 (時津): crews need the skill to swing the long, thin boat round quickly at the turn buoy.
- yellow-post / tabirai: crew roles also include a 采振り (signaller), and the hull is a long, thin bow shape (弓形).
- 長与町 広報: placings are decided when the bow (舳先) crosses the line.
